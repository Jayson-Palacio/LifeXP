## 🎬 Adobe Creative Suite Video Production Blueprint

With your full Adobe Creative Suite subscription, you can produce content that stands out from typical parenting videos. Use this editing checklist:

### Premiere Pro Workflow
1. **Dynamic Pacing**: Cut out all breathing pauses. Standard vertical pacing is 1.5 to 2.5 seconds per shot. Use quick zoom adjustments (100% to 110%) to highlight key phrases.
2. **Audio Sweetening**: 
   - Apply a high-pass filter to voiceovers to clean up low-end noise.
   - Use a clear "pop" or "clink" sound effect at 12dB whenever a mission checkmark appears.
   - Layer quiet, upbeat background music (e.g., Lofi Chill or positive acoustic beats) at -24dB.
3. **Thumbnail Export**: Use Photoshop to export high-contrast thumbnail images. Apply a subtle drop shadow to text and outline children/tablets with a thin glowing border.

### After Effects Assets
* **Coin Animation**: Create a reusable template of gold coins dropping down from the top of the frame. Overlay this during "level up" and "reward purchase" moments.
* **Text Presets**: Build a kinetic typography preset with a bounce expression for text captions to increase viewer watch time.
* **Color Grade**: Apply a LUT that brightens warm tones (gold/orange) and deepens dark navy backgrounds to match Kaeluma's color scheme.
