# 🔁 Organic Growth Loops & Advocacy Mechanics

> **Growth Strategy**: Because Kaeluma is 100% free with no advertising budget, we grow purely through word-of-mouth (virality) and community recommendations. Parents share tools that solve their problems. Our goal is to make sharing simple and rewarding.

---

## 1. The Kaeluma Referral Loop

Since the app is entirely free, we do not offer financial credits or premium months. Instead, we use a **Value-for-Value Social Loop**:

```
Parent experiences morning calm using Kaeluma
    → App prompts parent to "Share the Peace" after a 7-day streak
        → Parent copies invitation template to a local group chat (WhatsApp, Slack, PTA)
            → New parents sign up for free, ad-free routine help
                → New parent experiences success → repeat
```

---

## 2. Lever 1: "Share the Peace" Parent Templates

We place a quick-share widget in the **Parent Overview Dashboard Sidebar** and in the footer of the **Weekly Progress Emails** (sent via Resend).

### Custom Sharing Prompts (Parents can copy-paste in one click)

#### For Parent Group Chats (WhatsApp / iMessage / Messenger)
> *"Hey everyone, if anyone else is struggling to get their kids to brush their teeth and get dressed in the morning, check out Kaeluma (kaeluma.com). It's a free app built by a developer dad that turns routines into a quest board where kids earn gold coins. No ads, no subscriptions. It completely ended our morning arguments."*

#### For Social Media Posts (Facebook / Instagram)
> *"We started using a gold coin economy in our house to manage morning routines. The kids earn coins for their chores, and they use those coins to 'buy' their screen time. It's ad-free, completely free, and built by an indie dad. Highly recommend: kaeluma.com 🪙"*

---

## 3. Lever 2: Built-in Milestone Prompts

We prompt parents to share their kids' wins at key emotional milestones in the app:

| Milestone Event | Parent Emotional State | Prompt Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Kid Reaches Level 5** | Pride in child's consistency | Show celebration card: *"Your child is Level 5! Click here to download a custom accomplishment badge to share."* |
| **First Reward Redeemed** | Relief that the system works | Show popup: *"Your child just earned their first reward. Share this visual economy system with a friend."* |
| **7-Day Streak Achieved** | Excitement that the routine stuck | Nudge: *"7 days of morning routines without nagging. You've earned a coffee! Share the peace with another parent."* |

---

## 4. Lever 3: Niche Community Advocacy

### The Homeschool Co-op Circle
Homeschool parents actively exchange tips for daily schedules. 
* **The Play**: Reach out to homeschool Facebook group admins and offering Kaeluma as a free, ad-free schedule helper. Ask them to feature it in their resource list.

### The ADHD Support Network
ADHD parent groups (CHADD, local forums, ADHD subreddits) are constantly looking for visual routine charts. Because Kaeluma is free and ad-free, it is welcomed in these groups where paid apps are flagged as spam.
* **The Play**: Share Kaeluma as an open-source visual aid resource, explaining the neuro-benefits of immediate coin feedback loops.

## 6. Organic Growth KPIs Dashboard

Review these metrics weekly. If the Activation or Retention rates drop, stop marketing focus and fix the dashboard onboarding.

| Metric | Calculation | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Activation Rate** | Families who created ≥ 1 quest / Total signups | **≥ 50%** |
| **Day-7 Retention** | Families active at Day 7 / Families active at Day 1 | **≥ 45%** |
| **Day-14 Retention** | Families active at Day 14 / Families active at Day 1 | **≥ 30%** |
| **Donation Conversion Rate** | Stripe donation checkouts / Active family accounts | **≥ 3%** |
| **Viral Coefficient (K-Factor)** | (Invites sent per parent) × (Invite signup rate) | **≥ 0.3** |
| **Resend Open Rate** | Open emails / Delivered emails | **≥ 40%** |
