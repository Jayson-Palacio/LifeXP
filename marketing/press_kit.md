# 📰 Kaeluma Press Kit

> **Product**: Kaeluma turns your family's daily routines into a living RPG — kids earn gold coins and XP for real-life chores, then spend them in a parent-controlled reward shop.
> **Pricing**: 100% free with no ads or subscriptions, supported entirely by voluntary parent donations via Stripe.

## The One-Line Pitch
**"Kaeluma turns your family's daily chores into a living RPG, ending morning nagging through a free, ad-free, pay-what-you-can parenting tool."**

---

## The Problem We Solve

Every parent is familiar with the morning nagging loop: *"Brush your teeth... Did you brush your teeth? ...PLEASE brush your teeth."* It starts the day with conflict and stress for both parent and child. 

Traditional solutions (sticker charts, chore wheels, and allowances) quickly lose their novelty because they lack immediate feedback, visual progression, and compelling rewards. Kaeluma applies the same psychology that keeps kids engaged in games like Roblox to real-life household responsibilities.

---

## How It Works (3-Step Loop)

1. **Assign**: Parents set up "missions" with gold coin and XP values (e.g., "Get Dressed" = 5 gold, 15 XP).
2. **Play**: Kids log into their animated dashboard, check off completed quests, listen to coins clink, and watch their XP bar grow to level up.
3. **Redeem**: Kids spend their earned gold coins in the Parent-controlled Reward Shop (e.g., screen time, stay up 15 minutes late, choosing dinner).

## The Founder Story

Kaeluma was built by **Jayson Palacio**, a software developer, father, and the creator behind the children's YouTube show *Adventures with Amiga*. 

The idea came from a simple observation: his son would spend hours working toward virtual rewards in games but fought daily routine tasks tooth-and-nail.

> *"I realized I was the problem. I was asking him to do boring things for no immediate reward. So I changed the deal. I gave his morning routine XP and gold coins. By day three, he was checking off his quests before I even woke up. I used my kids' media experience to build an interface that feels like a game, not a homework list."*

Rather than charging a subscription, Jayson decided to make Kaeluma free for all families, relying on a value-for-value donation model to cover hosting costs.

## 🎙️ Journalist Q&A

**Q: What inspired you to build Kaeluma?**  
**A:** It was a personal breaking point. Every single morning began with nagging my 5-year-old son to brush his teeth and get dressed. I was starting the day angry, and he was starting it feeling defensive. I noticed he would spend hours playing games like Roblox, working hard for virtual coins. I thought: why not make his real-life morning routines feel like a quest? I built Kaeluma to turn chores into games, and the change was instant. On day three, he did his routine before I woke up.

**Q: Why is Kaeluma 100% free with no ads or subscriptions?**  
**A:** Subscription fatigue is real, and parenting apps shouldn't gate essential features behind paywalls. I wanted Kaeluma to be accessible to every family, regardless of their budget. We run Kaeluma on a voluntary, pay-what-you-can donation model. If the app saves your family's morning sanity, you can choose to support hosting costs. If not, you use it for free.

**Q: Is the app safe for children's privacy?**  
**A:** Absolutely. Privacy is our top priority. We do not display ads, do not track children, do not collect email addresses for child profiles, and do not sell any data. It is built on a secure Supabase backend and runs as a web app, meaning it doesn't require any invasive app store or device permissions.

**Q: How does this compare to traditional screen-time blocking apps?**  
**A:** Most parenting apps are punitive—they block screens, which creates resentment in kids. Kaeluma does the opposite: it turns screen time into an economy. Kids earn gold coins by doing real chores, then spend those coins to redeem screen time in the Parent Shop. It shifts control to the child, teaching them self-regulation and delayed gratification.
