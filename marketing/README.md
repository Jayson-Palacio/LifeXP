## 🎯 The 4 Metrics That Matter

Every marketing and product decision should optimize for these four metrics:

1. **Activation Rate** — Did a new family sign up and create at least 1 chore? (Target: ≥ 50%)
2. **Day-14 Retention** — Are families still using Kaeluma 2 weeks later? (Target: ≥ 30%)
3. **K-Factor (Word-of-Mouth)** — How many new families does each active parent bring in? (Target: ≥ 0.3)
4. **Donation Conversion Rate** — Percent of active parents who make a voluntary tip to support the app. (Target: ≥ 3%)

> [!TIP]
> Since Kaeluma has no paywalls or budget, focus entirely on **activation** and **retention**. Happy parents whose mornings are saved are the ones who share the app and donate to cover hosting.

## 🌟 Our Unfair Advantages

We aren't competing with VC-funded apps on advertising spend. We win through our unique strengths:

1. **True Origin Story**: Built by a developer dad for his own 5-year-old son to end morning routine chaos. It's real, emotional, and highly relatable.
2. **Adobe Suite & Video Production**: A full Adobe subscription combined with professional video editing experience means our TikTok, Reels, and Shorts will look like high-production media assets (custom graphics, After Effects overlays, Premiere pacing).
3. **Adventures with Amiga Audience**: We have a warm, pre-existing audience of families and kids who already trust our content team. We can tap this pool as an auxiliary booster.
4. **Pure Gift Economy**: Being 100% free with no ads makes us highly shareable in homeschool, ADHD parent, and teacher circles where paid trackers are heavily criticized.
