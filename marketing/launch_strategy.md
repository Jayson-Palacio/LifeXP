## 4. Value-for-Value Donation Economy

Kaeluma is built as a public utility for parents. There are no paywalls, no child profiles limits, and no locked color themes. 

### Why This Positioning Works
1. **Low Friction**: Parents are skeptical of adding another subscription. A free app has an incredibly low barrier to entry.
2. **High Shareability**: Parents, teachers, and support groups love recommending free resources. 
3. **Emotional Conversion**: When Kaeluma successfully ends morning yelling, parents feel a sense of gratitude. We trigger the donation request at this peak emotional moment (e.g., after a 7-day streak or first reward redemption).

### Donation Framing
* **Stripe Link**: `https://donate.stripe.com/28EfZg6aG81Of5zd8ggQE00`
* **Core Message**: *"Kaeluma has no paywalls or venture capital. It's built by a dad to help families. If it helped yours, consider tipping to keep the servers running."*

## 5. Target Personas

### Persona A: "The Overwhelmed Parent" 🥱
* **Who they are**: Parents of kids aged 4–10. Mornings are stressful. They've tried sticker charts, chore wheels, and raising their voice. Nothing works.
* **Their search intent**: "how to get kids to do chores without fighting," "morning routine checklist kids."
* **Kaeluma hook**: *"What if your kid did their morning routine before you even woke up?"*
* **Channel**: TikTok, Instagram Reels, parenting forums.

### Persona B: "The Gamer Parent" 🎮
* **Who they are**: Millennial dads and moms who grew up playing RPGs (Zelda, Pokémon, WoW). They love systems, levels, stats, and achievements.
* **Their search intent**: "gamified chore app for kids," "RPG chore tracker."
* **Kaeluma hook**: *"Finally, a chore system that speaks in XP and loot tables. Introduce your kid to the grind."*
* **Channel**: Reddit (r/gaming, r/parenting), Twitter/X (build-in-public), Indie Hackers.

### Persona C: "The ADHD Family" 🧠
* **Who they are**: Parents of children with ADHD, ASD, or executive dysfunction. Standard systems fail because the dopamine feedback is too slow.
* **Their search intent**: "ADHD chore chart visual," "dopamine routine app for kids."
* **Kaeluma hook**: *"ADHD brains need immediate rewards. Kaeluma's coin sounds and XP bars deliver."*
* **Channel**: ADHD subreddits (r/ADHD_parents, r/Parenting_ADHD), Facebook support groups.

### Persona D: "The Homeschool Family" 📚
* **Who they are**: Parents who homeschool. They need daily structure and daily habit completion, and they rely heavily on peer recommendations.
* **Their search intent**: "homeschool daily schedule structure," "motivate kids homeschool."
* **Kaeluma hook**: *"Give your homeschool day structure, rewards, and a progress system kids love."*
* **Channel**: Homeschool networks, co-ops, Pinterest.
